# Statutory Checksum Algorithms

A technical explanation of the mathematical error-detection algorithms used in EasyDP Local PII Scanner to eliminate false positives.

---

## 1. Aadhaar: Verhoeff Dihedral Group Algorithm ($D_5$)

Aadhaar numbers are 12 digits long. The 12th digit is a checksum generated by the **Verhoeff algorithm**, developed by Jacobus Verhoeff in 1969.

### Why Verhoeff?
Unlike simple Luhn checks (used in credit cards), Verhoeff:
- Detects **100% of all single-digit errors** (e.g., typing `3` instead of `8`).
- Detects **100% of all adjacent transposition errors** (e.g., typing `23` instead of `32`).

### Mathematical Structure:
The algorithm operates on the dihedral group $D_5$ (symmetries of a regular pentagon, isomorphic to permutations on 5 vertices):
- Multiplication table $D$ ($10 \times 10$)
- Permutation table $P$ ($8 \times 10$)
- Inversion table $inv$ ($10$)

```text
Validation formula:
c = 0
For each digit a_i from right to left (index i from 0):
    c = D[c][P[i % 8][a_i]]
Valid if c == 0
```

UIDAI specifications also mandate that legitimate Aadhaar numbers never begin with `0` or `1`.

## 2. GSTIN: ISO 7064 Mod-36 Check Digit

Goods and Services Tax Identification Numbers (GSTIN) are 15 characters long:
- Chars 1–2: State code (01 to 37, 97, 99)
- Chars 3–12: 10-character PAN of the taxpayer
- Char 13: Entity number (1–9, A–Z)
- Char 14: Default letter `'Z'`
- Char 15: Check digit

The 15th check digit is calculated using a **Mod-36 algorithm** over the character set `0-9A-Z` (values 0–35):
- Alternating weights factor ($1, 2, 1, 2, \dots$)
- Product quotient and remainder summation
- Modulo 36 complement check

## 3. PAN: 4th-Character Entity Classification

Permanent Account Numbers (PAN) follow a statutory 10-character layout: `[A-Z]{3}[PCHFATBLJG][A-Z][0-9]{4}[A-Z]`.

The 4th character determines the legal entity type under the Income Tax Act 1961:
- `P`: Individual (Person)
- `C`: Company
- `H`: Hindu Undivided Family (HUF)
- `F`: Firm / Limited Liability Partnership (LLP)
- `A`: Association of Persons (AOP)
- `T`: Trust
- `B`: Body of Individuals (BOI)
- `L`: Local Authority
- `J`: Artificial Juridical Person
- `G`: Government Agency
