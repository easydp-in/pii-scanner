B2B & Commercial Coverage

Full support for Indian B2B workflows and commercial taxation:

  • GSTIN: Mod-36 statutory check digit verification.
  • Entity-Classified PAN: Categorizes companies (C), partnership firms (F), trusts (T), and sole proprietors (P).
  • Bank IFSC Codes: RBI compliant routing verification.
🔐

B2C Statutory Checksums

Mathematical checksums eliminate false alarms on random numbers:

  • Aadhaar: Verhoeff Dihedral ($D_5$) check digit validation (rejects invalid prefixes starting with 0 or 1).
  • Indian Phone Numbers: National Numbering Plan with strict boundary isolation.
  • UPI VPA Handles: Detects handles across PhonePe, Google Pay, and Paytm.
📊

Tally ERP 9 & Prime Connector

Direct live audit of accounting databases without manual CSV exports:

  • Connects over local port 9000 loopback (127.0.0.1).
  • Audits Sundry Debtors and Sundry Creditors ledgers.
  • Identifies personal PANs and phone numbers in vendor accounts.
